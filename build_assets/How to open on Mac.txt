DESKTOP AQUARIUM FOR MAC

1. Drag "Desktop Aquarium" onto the Applications folder (in this window).

2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app
   for malware, because it isn't from the App Store. That's normal for a homemade game.
   Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash"), then:

     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security
     - Scroll down to "Desktop Aquarium was blocked..."
     - Click "Open Anyway" and type your Mac password

   (On older macOS you can instead right-click the app > Open > Open.)
   You only need to do this once.

3. The tank sits at the bottom-right of your screen, above the Dock.
   Updates install themselves from inside the game (Options > Check for updates).

Your save lives in ~/Library/Application Support/DesktopAquarium/
