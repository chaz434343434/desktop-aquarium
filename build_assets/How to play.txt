DESKTOP AQUARIUM  (version 1.5.1)
A cozy catfish aquarium that lives at the bottom of your screen.

STARTING IT
  Double-click DesktopAquarium.exe (keep it in this folder with the _internal folder).
  If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway".
  That warning shows up for any small homemade program that isn't from a big company.

THE BASICS
  - Click a catfish to pet it. You get scales (the money), and it gets happier.
  - Pop the floating bubbles - click each one. Golden ones are worth 10x.
  - Feed your fish with the food buttons next to your scales.
  - Open the Shop to buy new catfish, toys, upgrades and tank themes.
  - Fish play with toys and shed scales when they do.
  - Hover over a fish to see how it's doing.
  - Fin Scratches (Shop > Upgrades) gives you a Scratch! button. Press it, then hold the mouse
    down on your fish and wiggle: every scratch pays! Lasts 3 seconds, +1 second per level
    (up to 15), then it recharges for a minute.
  - Right-click a fish to give it a name. To take a name off, right-click it again and
    press "Remove name" (or save an empty name).

WHEN YOU COME BACK
  - Build the Scale Jar (Shop > Upgrades). It fills up while you're away. Click it to collect.
  - Build the Hatchery and lay eggs (free). Their timers keep going even when the game is closed. When they hatch, every fish grows - and sometimes
    a baby catfish hatches too! It starts as a tiny fry and grows up over a few minutes. Small Eggs can also hatch nothing at all.
  - If a baby hatches and your tank is full, you must sell a fish (or the baby) to continue.
  - Golden Eggs are 5x as likely to hatch a SHINY baby.
  - Golden bubbles and visitors sometimes drop a Mystery Egg. Lay it and see what hatches!
  - The Auto-Nest upgrade lays your eggs again for you after they hatch. Switch it off in the
    Hatchery tab to pick different eggs, or press the x on an egg to take it out of the nest.
  - The Hatchery and My Tank buttons (next to Shop) jump straight to those tabs.
  - Look out for visitors (a crab, a shrimp, a turtle... even an axolotl). Click them for a gift.
  - The gold "Collect !" button grabs everything that's ready at once.
  - Drag the Scale Jar, the egg nest and the toys to move them around.

HOLIDAY SPECIALS
  - Some things are only for sale during a holiday (by your computer's date). They have their
    own tab in the Shop, named after the holiday (the orange "Halloween" tab).
    Halloween (October 1 - November 2): the Haunted Lagoon theme, the Pumpkin Catfish,
    4 spooky toys (Jack-o'-Lantern, Witch's Cauldron, Fishbone Skeleton, Candy Corn) and
    3 upgrades (Ghostly Glow, Spooky Bubbles, Trick-or-Treat).
  - The Haunted Lagoon has gravestones, glowing jack-o'-lanterns and a friendly ghost.
    Its pop-bubbles are skulls worth 2x, and it gives +10% scales forever.
  - The Pumpkin Catfish earns twice as much as your best catfish and blows skull bubbles
    worth 3x. One per tank.
  - Once you've bought them, they're yours: the theme stays, and the Pumpkin Catfish, toys and
    upgrades can be bought again after a Spawning Season, any time of year.

THE LONG GAME
  - Later on, Shop > Pearls lets you start a new Spawning Season. You lose your fish and
    upgrades, but earn Pearls.
  - Spend Pearls in the Pearl Tree for bonuses that last forever: more scales, faster eggs,
    a head start, luckier bubbles and more.
  - There are 60 catfish to collect, including the Blue Channel Catfish, drawn by a friend.
    After the Moonlight Catfish, giant river catfish and
    mythical ones (Aurora, Galaxy) appear, along with late-game toys and upgrades that unlock
    as your tank gets richer.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - It never steals your keyboard or mouse from other programs, so it can sit there all day.
  - Clicks on the empty space around the tank go straight through to whatever is behind it.
    (On a Mac the tank sits in its own small window instead, which grows while the shop is open.)
  - "Chill" pauses hunger and happiness if you just want to watch.
  - Options lets you change the tank size, position, brightness, monitor, sound and more.
  - Text too small? Options > UI size makes all the buttons and text 150% or 200% bigger.
  - Computer lagging? Options > Performance > Battery saver makes the tank even lighter.
    The game also slows itself down while a full-screen video or game is in front of it.
  - Drag the dotted bar at the top of the Shop/Fishdex to move it.
  - The game saves by itself. Click the red x on the tank to save and close.
  - Found a bug or need help? Options > Bugs & Support opens GitHub in your browser.
  - Updates: when a new version is out, an "Update!" button appears. Click it and the game
    updates itself and reopens. Your fish are kept.
    You can also check any time: Options > Updates > "Check for updates".

Have fun, and happy fishkeeping!
